# Research Methodology

General principles for conducting research in this agent system.

## Source Hierarchy

1. Official documentation and specifications
2. Peer-reviewed papers and formal reports
3. Verified data sources (on-chain data, official APIs)
4. Reputable industry publications
5. Blog posts from known domain experts
6. Community discussions (with caveats noted)

## Domain-Specific Notes

### Crypto / DeFi
- On-chain data is ground truth; off-chain analysis is interpretation
- Protocol documentation > third-party explainers
- Governance proposals and forum discussions are primary sources for protocol direction
- Dune Analytics has ~30 min lag; RPC indexing for real-time needs

### AI / ML
- Arxiv preprints are acceptable but note peer review status
- Model benchmarks from the releasing org may be optimistic; prefer independent evals
- Rapidly evolving field; sources older than 6 months may be outdated

## Research Integrity

- Never fabricate or embellish sources
- If a search returns no results, say so
- Distinguish between "no evidence found" and "evidence of absence"
- When sources conflict, present both sides with context
